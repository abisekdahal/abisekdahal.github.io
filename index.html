About & Research Focus

Targeted domains in ubiquitous computing, machine learning, and sensor intelligence.

Dr. Abisek Dahal currently serves as an Assistant Professor (Grade-II, Pay Level 10) in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Surat (IIIT Surat), having joined in July 2026.

He completed his Doctorate (Ph.D.) in Computer Science & Engineering from National Institute of Technology Meghalaya with an outstanding coursework GPA of 9.70/10. His doctoral research focused on designing efficient machine learning and hybrid deep learning architectures for sensor-based Human Activity Recognition (HAR) operating in real-world, uncontrolled environments.

His active research portfolio bridges edge-assisted deep learning, federated privacy-preserving models, multimodal sensor fusion, and lightweight trajectory optimization algorithms for unmanned aerial vehicles in emergency response scenarios.
