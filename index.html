Academic Expertise

Research Interests & Focus Areas

Bridging Intelligent Sensing, Machine Learning, and Ubiquitous Computing for Smart Healthcare and Edge Infrastructure.

Wearable Computing

Low-power sensor integration, body-worn IMUs, signal preprocessing, and feature engineering for continuous real-time physiological and kinematic monitoring.

Human Activity Recognition (HAR)

Designing hybrid deep learning architectures and robust algorithms to tackle uncontrolled, noise-heavy, real-world sensor streams and subject variability.

Machine & Federated Learning

Privacy-preserving decentralized ML models, on-device training, lightweight neural networks, and domain adaptation across heterogeneous devices.

e-Healthcare & Smart Systems

Pervasive patient health tracking, fall detection systems, automated risk diagnostic pipelines, and intelligent medical IoT integrations.

Edge Placement & 5G/6G Networks

Edge server placement, latency minimization algorithms, computational offloading, and resource allocation in ultra-reliable low-latency networks.

UAV Path Planning & Disasters

Unmanned Aerial Vehicle trajectory optimization, energy-efficient data collection models, and emergency routing across disaster-affected zones.
